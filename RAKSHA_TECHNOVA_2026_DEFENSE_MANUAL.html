🎯 THE CORE THESIS — Memorise This. It Anchors Everything.

India's existing fraud defences stop at the phone number. Raksha starts where they end — inside the live conversation — using real-time neural intent classification to detect psychological coercion as it unfolds, physically interrupt the victim's trance, and dispatch cryptographic proof to family and police before money leaves the account.

PART II

Complete Alignment with All 11 Assessment Criteria

Criterion 1 — Problem Identification & National Relevance

What the jury wants to hear: Clarity of the problem; importance to India; clearly identified users/beneficiaries; urgency and scale.

India is experiencing an epidemic of conversational financial fraud — not phishing links, not malware, but live human-to-human psychological manipulation over phone and video calls.

The dominant attack pattern is called "Digital Arrest": a caller impersonates CBI, Customs, TRAI, or RBI; claims the victim's Aadhaar or PAN is linked to money laundering or narcotics trafficking; places the victim under a fabricated "digital arrest" where they must remain on video call for hours; then coerces them into transferring their savings to a "verification account."

The Scale is Staggering

  • The Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C) under MHA reported that Indians lost ₹1,750 crore to cyber fraud in the first 4 months of 2024 alone. The 2025 figure is projected to exceed ₹11,333 crore.
  • The National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) recorded 65,893 cybercrime cases in 2022 — a 24.4% year-on-year rise.
  • RBI's 2024 Annual Report flagged digital fraud as the single fastest-growing threat to financial inclusion.
  • PM Modi dedicated an entire segment of Mann Ki Baat (October 2024) specifically to warning citizens about digital arrest scams.

Why Existing Tools Fail

ToolWhat It DoesWhy It Fails Against Digital Arrest
TruecallerLooks up caller number in a crowdsourced databaseAttacker uses a 2-day-old SIM or WhatsApp — number has zero reports yet
Airtel AI Spam FilterBlocks bulk SMS and known robo-callers at network levelCannot inspect the content of a peer-to-peer voice/video call (carrier-grade encryption)
Google Phone Call ScreenTranscribes the first 10 seconds before you pick upDoes nothing after you answer — the scam unfolds over 45–180 minutes of live conversation
UPI Daily LimitsCaps transfer amountsA panicked victim will make multiple transactions within the limit, or switch to NEFT/RTGS

The Structural Gap

Every existing defence operates either before the call connects (caller ID) or after the money is gone (bank complaints, police FIR). There is no product in India — or globally — that actively monitors the live conversation for coercion patterns and intervenes in real time. Raksha fills this gap.

Who Are the Victims?

  • Primary: Senior citizens and retirees (60+ years), who hold significant savings, are unfamiliar with digital verification, and are psychologically vulnerable to authority impersonation.
  • Secondary: First-generation digital banking users in Tier 2/3 cities who transact via UPI but lack cyber literacy.
  • Tertiary: Working professionals aged 25–45 who are the protectors — they want a way to shield their parents remotely.

Criterion 2 — User Understanding & Problem Validation

What the jury wants to hear: Evidence from users, field research, credible data; understanding of existing solutions and their limitations.

Stream 1 — Published Victim Testimony Analysis

We studied 50+ FIR narratives published by Tamil Nadu and Karnataka Cyber Crime Cells. Every single digital arrest case followed a near-identical 5-stage coercion script:

StageAttacker's ScriptPsychological Mechanism
1. CONTACT"This is Inspector Sharma, Mumbai Cyber Crime Branch"Authority priming — victim defers to perceived government power
2. ALLEGATION"Your Aadhaar is linked to 14kg of MDMA seized at Mumbai airport"Fear induction — absurd but terrifying claim
3. ISOLATION"Do not tell your family or you will be arrested for obstruction"Social isolation — removes the victim's safety net

This invariant structure is precisely what makes AI detection feasible. Scammers must follow these stages because the psychology of coercion requires them in sequence. You cannot demand money from a stranger without first establishing false authority and inducing fear.

Stream 2 — Direct User Surveys (n=140+)

  • 78% had received at least one impersonation call in the past 6 months.
  • 92% reported feeling "panicked and unable to think clearly" during a high-pressure call.
  • 84% of respondents aged 55+ could not explain how to verify whether a call was genuinely from police or CBI.
  • 96% said they would want a tool that alerts their family if a suspicious call is detected.

Stream 3 — Competitive Gap Analysis

We tested the top 4 defence tools against a fresh VoIP number making a simulated scam call: Truecaller — No warning. Google Phone — No screening after call answered. Samsung Smart Call — No warning. Jio Spam Filter — No warning.

Result: 4/4 existing tools failed to provide any protection during the call itself. This is not a failure of those products — it is a category that does not yet exist. Raksha creates it.

Criterion 3 — Solution Concept & Innovation

What the jury wants to hear: Originality; improvement over alternatives; creativity; strength of the value proposition.

Raksha introduces a fundamentally new category of protection: Active In-Call Behavioural Defence.

1. Turn-by-Turn Semantic Stage Tracking

Unlike spam filters that make a single binary decision (spam / not spam) at call initiation, Raksha maintains a running state machine across the entire conversation. Each utterance is classified into a coercion stage. The system only escalates when it observes a sustained trajectory of manipulation — not a single keyword.

2. Manipulation Velocity — A Novel Metric

Vmanipulation = Δ(coercion stage) / Δt

A legitimate institutional call has low velocity — the representative is patient. A scam call exhibits high velocity — the attacker must move the victim through fear → isolation → payment within minutes. Velocity is the discriminant.

3. The Physical Safety Brake

This is not a notification. It is a full-screen, high-contrast, un-dismissible overlay that physically breaks the victim's visual and cognitive engagement with the attacker's instructions:

  • 🛑 STOP. DO NOT TRANSFER MONEY.
  • ⚠️ DIGITAL ARREST SCAM DETECTED
  • 🔒 VERIFY WITH YOUR FAMILY BEFORE PROCEEDING

4. Zero-Touch Family Emergency Dispatch

Simultaneously with the Safety Brake, Raksha fires an emergency payload to a pre-configured trusted contact containing: alert type, timestamp, and cryptographic hash of the detected scam transcript.

5. Tamper-Proof Evidence Chain

Hn = SHA-256( Hn-1 ‖ eventn ‖ timestampn )

This creates an immutable, ordered, cryptographically verifiable ledger that can be submitted directly to cybercrime.gov.in or used by banks to process rapid fund-recovery requests.

Criterion 4 — Relevance & Depth of AI Application

What the jury wants to hear: Whether AI is genuinely required; appropriate use of ML/NLP; intelligence created beyond simple automation.

Why AI is Non-Negotiable

Consider: A: "RBI employees will never ask for your OTP." vs B: "I am from RBI. Share your OTP for verification." Both contain "RBI," "OTP," and references to sharing credentials. A keyword filter would either flag both (unacceptable false positives) or miss both. The difference is semantic intent and contextual role assignment — which is what neural language models capture.

Layer 1 — Acoustic Feature Extraction & Multilingual ASR

Handles code-switching within utterances (Hinglish, Tanglish) and normalises into clean text for downstream analysis.

Layer 2 — PyTorch Semantic Transformer Classifier (v2)

Classifies each turn into: scam probability (0.0–1.0), dominant manipulation tactic, and progression stage. Achieved: Precision: 94.2% | Recall: 96.8% | F1: 0.955

Layer 3 — Multi-Turn State Progression Machine

Aggregates per-turn predictions to compute cumulative risk trajectory, manipulation velocity, and stage-gate conditions. The Safety Brake never fires on a single utterance — it requires a sustained, escalating pattern.

Layer 4 — Visual Threat Intelligence (Raksha Video)

Lightweight YOLOv11 edge model for: institutional badge/uniform detection, document forgery indicators, and facial consistency analysis (deepfake detection).

Intelligence Created

Raksha creates real-time coercion trajectory intelligence — the ability to determine, within 3–5 conversational turns and under 450ms latency, that a live phone call is a psychologically manipulative extortion attempt, and to act before irreversible financial harm occurs.

Criterion 8 — Responsible AI, Privacy, Security & Inclusiveness

1. Zero Audio Retention

Audio is processed in ephemeral memory buffers and immediately discarded. The backend receives only text tokens — never WAV, MP3, or PCM samples.

2. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

  • Explicit Consent: Protection only activates on manual "Start Protection" tap with persistent notification.
  • Purpose Limitation: Data used solely for fraud detection. Never sold or used for profiling.
  • Right to Erasure: Users can delete all session history from app settings.

3. Cryptographic Evidence Integrity

SHA-256 hash chain ensures evidence cannot be fabricated or tampered with. Protects both the user (proving coercion) and the accused (proving evidence was not planted).

4. Bias Management

Training data balanced across multiple languages, scam archetypes, and legitimate scenarios. Specific audits for demographic bias to prevent disproportionate flagging based on accents or dialects.

5. Inclusivity & Accessibility

  • Large-text, high-contrast Safety Brake UI for elderly users.
  • Regional language roadmap covering 8 scheduled languages.
  • One-button operation — minimal technical literacy required.

6. Explainability

The Safety Brake explains why it fired: "Scammer claimed to be from a government agency (Authority Tactic)" — educating users about manipulation techniques.

Checklist 6 — Jury Rapid-Fire Defense (All 8 Questions)

Q1: "What makes your solution different from Truecaller, Google Phone, or UPI limits?"

"Existing solutions answer: 'Who is calling me?' Raksha answers: 'What is being done to me during this call?'

Truecaller checks a phone number database before you pick up. If the number is clean — and it always is in targeted scams — the victim is on their own. Raksha activates after the call connects. It semantically analyses every conversational turn, tracks the escalation trajectory across five psychological coercion stages, and autonomously triggers a physical Safety Brake and family emergency dispatch.

It's the difference between a security guard who checks IDs at the entrance versus an AI that detects a robbery in progress and hits the silent alarm."

Q2: "What happens if your AI model fails?"

"We design for failure at three levels:

Level 1 — False Positive: The Safety Brake requires multi-gate activation — sustained escalation across stages AND velocity threshold. Even if triggered falsely, it's an overlay, not a call termination. The user dismisses and continues.

Level 2 — False Negative: If the neural classifier misses novel phrasing, our deterministic heuristic stage machine monitors structural patterns — any caller demanding secrecy + money + credentials triggers rule-based escalation.

Level 3 — Network Failure: Falls back to on-device lightweight keyword detection. Never silently stops protecting."

Q3: "How do you handle privacy with live audio?"

"1. Zero audio storage — processed in ephemeral RAM, never written to disk. 2. Text-only backend transmission — server never receives raw speech. 3. Explicit consent — 'Start Protection' tap with visible foreground notification. 4. Client-side evidence hashing — SHA-256 on-device. 5. Full DPDP Act 2023 alignment."

Q4: "How accurate is your solution?"

"Held-out test set: Precision 94.2%, Recall 96.8%, F1 0.955. False positive rate on legitimate banking calls: 0.0%. Achievable because scam calls follow highly constrained linguistic scripts — the structural invariance makes ML classification effective. Honest limitation: accuracy may drop on unsupported languages."

Q5: "What is your implementation cost?"

"Server compute: ₹0.08 per call. 10,000 concurrent users: single $20/month VM. Edge deployment roadmap eliminates server dependency. Play Store: one-time $25. Total monthly cost at 50K users: ~₹40,000. Not GPU-hungry generative AI — lightweight text classification."

Q6: "Who are your competitors?"

"Tier 1 — Caller ID (Truecaller, Hiya): Blind once user answers. Tier 2 — Telecom Filters (Airtel, Jio): Cannot inspect encrypted peer-to-peer calls. Tier 3 — Banking Fraud (Visa/MC AI, UPI rules): Flag anomalous transactions, but victim willingly initiates transfer under coercion. Raksha occupies an entirely uncontested space: real-time, in-call, content-level behavioural defence with autonomous intervention."
